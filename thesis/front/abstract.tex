\begin{cabstract}
本文面向中性原子量子处理节点的网络化需求，研究光子—原子量子接口的第一性原理建模与端到端性能评估问题。针对传统经验模型难以统一处理“发射—频率转换—传输—干涉—探测”耦合链路的局限，本文建立了基于时间 bin 离散化与矩阵乘积态（MPS）的统一仿真框架。理论上，从 QED 有效化出发构造四能级原子—腔发射模型，并给出 5 维 QFC 门与 5×3 维滤波记忆门的显式张量表达；在测量端采用 Heisenberg 对偶映射，将光纤损耗、偏振与相位噪声、探测效率及暗计数一致推回 POVM effect，从而在保持态端纯态演化的同时保留完整统计。算法上，框架以 TEBD 实现逐 bin 局域更新和可控截断，输出成功率、真假成功分解、条件保真度与 CHSH 等指标。参数设置采用实验锚点口径，并通过分层标定路径连接中间可观测量与终态任务量。结果表明，该方法可在可控算力下完成百时间 bin 规模的端到端评估，并为接口参数优化、误差预算和方案比较提供可解释、可复现的量化依据。
\end{cabstract}

\begin{eabstract}
This thesis studies first-principles modeling and end-to-end performance evaluation of photon--atom quantum interfaces for networked neutral-atom processing nodes. To overcome the limitations of empirical link-budget models in handling the coupled chain of emission, frequency conversion, transmission, interference, and detection, we develop a unified framework based on time-bin discretization and matrix product states (MPS). Starting from an effective QED reduction, we construct a four-level atom--cavity emission model and derive explicit tensor representations for a 5D QFC gate and a 5D$\times$3D filter-memory gate. On the measurement side, a Heisenberg-adjoint treatment pushes fiber loss, polarization/phase noise, detector efficiency, and dark counts back to POVM effects, preserving full detection statistics while keeping state-side evolution pure. Numerically, TEBD-based per-bin local updates with controlled truncation are used to compute heralding probability, true/false herald decomposition, conditional fidelity, and CHSH metrics. Parameters are anchored to experimental observables and calibrated through a layered pipeline from intermediate observables to end-task metrics. The framework enables end-to-end simulations at the hundred-bin scale under practical computational resources, and provides an interpretable and reproducible tool for parameter optimization, error budgeting, and cross-scheme comparison of quantum interfaces.
\end{eabstract}
