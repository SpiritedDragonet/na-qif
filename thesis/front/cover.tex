\begin{cabstract}
在量子网络与分布式量子计算中，远程纠缠是连接空间分离量子寄存器并支撑跨节点门操作、资源注入和网络协议的基础资源。中性原子节点兼具可编程原子寄存器和光学接口潜力，是研究远程纠缠链路的代表性平台。实际光子—原子接口链路同时受到原子发射波包、量子频率转换噪声、光纤损耗与偏振演化、两光子干涉、背景光子和探测器暗计数等因素影响，成功率与条件态保真度的损失来源难以仅由经验参数判断。围绕 Rb 原子、光学腔、量子频率转换、电信光纤和中心站 Bell 态测量构成的典型链路，建立可追溯的链路仿真与误差预算方法，有助于把器件参数、测量记录和末端纠缠质量放在统一评价口径下分析。

研究首先基于时间分箱离散化和矩阵乘积态（MPS）张量网络，把原子发射与偏振映射、量子频率转换（QFC）及滤波记忆效应、光纤传播、分束干涉、Bell 态测量和探测器响应统一表示为离散时间序列上的量子演化与条件化测量过程；随后采用时间演化块解耦算法（TEBD）实现局域门演化与交换门（SWAP）传送带调度，并结合量子轨迹采样和 Kraus 算符投影枚举成功探测记录，重建成功条件下的原子约化密度矩阵，计算宣告成功概率、真/假成功分量、条件保真度和 CHSH 指标。基于公开实验参数完成波包、窗口、HOM 干涉、QFC/探测器工作平面和距离扩展分析后，结果表明：$70\,\mathrm{ns}$ 数据接收窗口给出较均衡的速率—质量工作点；推荐分束器参数约位于 $\theta_{\mathrm{BS}}\approx0.75\,\mathrm{rad}$；假成功主要由背景光子和探测器暗计数共同贡献，其中背景辅助略占主导；真成功事件更集中于小时间差记录；链路距离增加时成功率先于条件态质量退化，长距离场景首先受到有效宣告事件数限制。上述结果把末端保真度损失拆解为窗口选择、干涉质量、背景噪声和暗计数等可操作因素，为接口参数筛选和误差预算提供了依据。\end{cabstract}

\begin{eabstract}
In quantum networks and distributed quantum computing, remote entanglement is a basic resource for connecting spatially separated quantum registers and supporting inter-node gates, resource injection, and network protocols. Neutral-atom nodes combine programmable atomic registers with optical-interface capabilities, making them a representative platform for studying remote-entanglement links. In practical photon--atom interface links, the success probability and conditional-state fidelity are jointly affected by atomic wave-packet emission, quantum-frequency-conversion noise, fiber loss and polarization evolution, two-photon interference, background photons, and detector dark counts. These coupled factors make it difficult to identify fidelity-loss mechanisms from empirical parameters alone. This work focuses on a representative link consisting of Rb atoms, an optical cavity, quantum frequency conversion, telecom fiber, and central-station Bell-state measurement, and develops a traceable link-simulation and error-budgeting method that evaluates device parameters, detection records, and final entanglement quality within one consistent framework.

The study first formulates atomic emission and polarization mapping, quantum frequency conversion (QFC) with filtering memory, fiber propagation, beam-splitter interference, Bell-state measurement, and detector response as quantum evolution and conditioned measurement over a time-bin sequence using matrix product state (MPS) tensor networks. The time-evolving block decimation (TEBD) algorithm is then used for local-gate evolution and swap-gate (SWAP) conveyor-belt scheduling, while quantum trajectory sampling and Kraus-operator projection enumerate successful detection records. For each successful record set, the atomic reduced density matrix is reconstructed to evaluate the heralding success probability, true- and false-success components, conditional fidelity, and CHSH parameter. Parameter anchoring, window scanning, HOM-interference fitting, QFC/detector working-plane analysis, and distance-scaling simulations based on published experimental parameters show that a $70\,\mathrm{ns}$ data-acceptance window gives a balanced rate--quality operating point; the recommended beam-splitter setting is near $\theta_{\mathrm{BS}}\approx0.75\,\mathrm{rad}$; false successes are mainly caused by background photons and detector dark counts, with the background-assisted component slightly dominant; true successes concentrate more strongly in small time-difference records; and, as link distance increases, the success rate degrades before the conditional-state quality, so long-distance operation is first limited by the number of usable heralded events. These results decompose the final fidelity loss into operable factors including window selection, interference quality, background noise, and dark counts, providing a basis for interface-parameter screening and error budgeting.
\end{eabstract}
